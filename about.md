# About Cat Love Toys

Cat Love Toys is a small, honest cat-toy review and comparison site — no filler, no generic "top 10" listicles for the sake of it. Every category (toys first, with furniture, food, treats, and litter to come) is organized the way cat lovers actually shop, so you can find what you need without wading through noise.

The site is named, in spirit, after one specific cat.
